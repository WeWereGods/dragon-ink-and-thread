Holiday Fabric

Shopping list · fat quarters · for the swatch book, not for stock
FAT QUARTERS ONLY. DO NOT BUY YARDAGE. Stockings and the rest of the festive work are custom — the customer picks the fabric and then you buy the yardage for their piece. What you need in hand is something to show. ⏳ Sewing stops around Nov 7 and the move is Nov 25, so a yard bought today and not sewn is a box you drive to Virginia.
Already in the cupboard — don't buy these again: red metallic snowflake (the stocking) · Bowknots in Scarlet · Bowknots in Cream · Traditional Christmas · Solstice Tartan · cream plush · Toffee Plaid · Toffee Windowpane. ⚠️ Most of these are NOT in the fabric library yet — photograph them and they count as range without costing anything.

Prints eight fat quarters

What to look forWhy it earns a placePaid
Evergreen / pine tonal You have no green at all. Half of Christmas is green.
Green buffalo check or plaid The “for him” fabric, and exactly what the gift card holders need. Different enough from Toffee Plaid.
Christmas trees Most asked-for motif after snowflakes, and it reads at thumbnail size in the swatch book.
Gingerbread or cocoa The whimsical, kid-facing end — and it rhymes with The Cup and Cozy.
Candy cane stripe or nutcracker Unmistakably Christmas, and a stripe is a counterweight to a library full of florals.
Cream and gold winter The elegant non-red option. Plenty of people want a neutral stocking and you cannot offer one.
Midnight navy, gold stars Winter rather than Christmas — sells past December, and the most on-brand print on this list.
Holly or poinsettia botanical Bridges into Florals & Botanicals, which is the deepest collection you have.

Cuffs a quarter yard each — NOT fat quarters

Dark green plush A cuff needs a strip across the full width, so a fat quarter will not do.
Oatmeal sherpa or faux fur ⚠️ The cream plush shows every fingerprint. Anything living in a basket wants a darker cuff.

Supplies

Fusible interfacing, 1 yd Medium weight, Pellon SF101 or similar. This is the fix for the stocking being soft — it also goes into book sleeves and gift card holders.
