Make the pillow live. You cannot reshoot the bag, but you can sew one pillow on camera in two minutes. That shot is the whole proof — it is the difference between “nice bag” and “oh, that’s why.” Everything else is decoration.
Do not apologise for the price, explain it. Hook 3 does that outright. Price-justification videos travel, because everyone watching has silently thought it about someone else’s work. Post before a market and change the last line to “and I’ll have it with me this weekend” — then it is working for the table as well as the website.
