Market Half Apron

Waist apron · three pockets · cut for a 40″ waist, 51″ hip
27½″wide
14″long
22″band span
39″each tie
½″seam allowance

Cut list

PieceCutNotes
Apron body · fall print1 × 28½″ × 15½″ Hip ÷ 2 + 2″, plus hems. Covers the lap when seated and reaches the side seams.
Pocket · cream or contrast1 × 22″ × 9″ Finishes about 7½″ deep — a phone sits below the top edge.
Waistband · cream1 × 5″ × 23″ Waist ÷ 2 + 2″ = a 22″ span; folds to 2″ tall.
Ties · cream2 × 5″ × 40″ Waist ÷ 2 + 20″ — long enough to cross the back and knot at the FRONT.
Fabric: about ½ yard of the print and 1 yard of cream at 42–44″ wide. Cut the ties along the length of the cream so they need no piecing.

Layout

Sewing it

  1. Hem the pocket top: fold the long top edge over ½″, press, fold ½″ again, press and stitch. Press ½″ to the wrong side on the other three edges.
  2. Hem the apron body: a doubled ½″ hem down both sides, then a doubled 1″ hem across the bottom. Leave the top edge raw — the waistband takes it.
  3. Place the pocket centred, with its bottom edge 2″ up from the hemmed bottom. Pin, then stitch down one side, across the bottom and up the other, backstitching at both top corners — that is where a pocket tears.
  4. Divide the pocket with two lines of stitching, 7″ in from each side. That gives you three pockets: phone, pen and scissors, spare tags.
  5. Make the ties: fold each strip in half lengthwise, right sides together, and sew the long edge and one short end at an angle. Turn right side out, press flat.
  6. Gather the body: press ½″ to the wrong side along both long edges of the waistband. Pleat or gather the apron’s raw top edge until it measures 22″ (the band span). Three 1″ box pleats look tidier than gathers and sit flatter under a bum bag.
  7. Sandwich and stitch: tuck the gathered top edge inside the folded waistband, slide a raw tie end into each open end, pin everything, then topstitch right around the band close to the edge. That one line of stitching closes the band, catches the apron and anchors the ties.
  8. Press, and knot the ties at the front. Long ties tied in front photograph better than a bow at the back, and they keep the knot off a chair.
Two things worth doing: stitch a small loop of twill tape into the right-hand band seam for scissors, and interface the waistband if the cream is thin — a loaded apron sags at the waist first. These numbers are YOURS (40″ waist, 51″ hip). For any other body: body = hip ÷ 2 + 2″, band span = waist ÷ 2 + 2″, each tie = waist ÷ 2 + 20″.
