Order of work as far as the photos show

  1. Piece the patches into rows, press the seams in opposite directions row to row so they nest, then join the rows.
  2. Two faces for the cover, one panel for the mat. Press everything flat before cutting anything.
  3. Cut the cover faces to the dome — straight sides, a generous curve across the top. Sharp corners will not turn.
  4. Layer each piece with whatever goes inside (write it in above) and quilt or baste.
  5. Cover: right sides together, sew the curve, clip it, turn.
  6. Bind the hem in cream, and bind the mat all round.
  7. Press. It is what makes it look bought.
