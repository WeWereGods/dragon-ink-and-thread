⬜ CONFIRM THE ¼″ ONCE — and do not assume it from another sheet. 4½″ − ½″ = 4″ and 3½″ − ½″ = 3″, which takes a gift card (3⅜″ × 2⅛″) with ⅝″ of clearance. At ½″ it finishes 3½″ × 2½″ and the clearance falls to ⅛″ — the card goes in, and fights you every time. ⚠️ The tea mat and the scrunchie are ½″; the bow and this are ¼″. PieceCutWhat it becomes

Order of work

  1. Press all six pieces. They are small; a crease you sew in stays in.
  2. Fold each 3″ square in half on the diagonal, wrong sides together, and press hard. Four triangles.
  3. Lay one body piece RIGHT SIDE UP. This is the front.
  4. Set a triangle into each corner, raw edges flush with the body’s raw edges, folded edges pointing in to the middle. They will overlap in the centre — that overlap is the diamond. Pin or clip.
  5. Baste round the outside just inside ¼″ to stop everything shifting. On pieces this small it is faster than fighting pins.
  6. Lay the second body piece RIGHT SIDE DOWN on top. Sew all four sides at ¼″.
  7. Clip the four corners on the diagonal, close to the stitching.
  8. Turn it through the diamond. ⬜ Confirm this is how you do it — the photo shows no closed gap, which means the opening does the job and there is nothing to hand-stitch.
  9. Press flat, rolling the seams so neither fabric shows on the other’s face. Square the corners with a chopstick.
⬜ Calculated, not measured: a fat quarter of body fabric gives 20 pieces = 10 holders; a fat quarter of contrast gives 42 squares, also about 10. So one fat quarter of each makes ten.
⚠️ THIS IS THE SCRAP PRODUCT, AND THAT MATTERS RIGHT NOW. Nothing here is bigger than 3½″ × 4½″. That is what is left over from a bow, a bandana or a scrunchie — pieces that are otherwise too small to be anything.
⏳ Sewing stops around Nov 7 and the move is Nov 25. Every scrap sewn into one of these is a scrap you do not box and drive to Virginia. This is the one product where the move is an argument for making them.
Use the contrast. The four corners are the whole design — they are what anyone sees. A plain or small-scale print for the corners against a busier body reads better than two busy prints fighting. 📌 A 3″ square shows almost no pattern, so a large-scale print is wasted there; save those for the body.

Watch for

Press the diagonal folds HARD. A soft fold makes the diamond look accidental rather than designed. It is the only decorative element on the piece.
Get the four triangles even. The eye goes straight to the centre, and an off-centre diamond is the one thing that reads as homemade.
Clip the corners properly. Four corners on a 4″ piece is a lot of bulk in a small space.
Check a real card goes in before you make ten.
