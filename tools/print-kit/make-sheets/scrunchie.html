A fat quarterNONE ⚠️ It is 22″ long and you need 26″. This catches everyone once.
Cut the strips ALONG the 44″ width, five inches apart, and you take 26″ off a 36″ yard. Eight strips is 40″ of the 44″. 📌 The 10″ remnant is not waste — it is exactly the width for whimsys at 3″, so a yard of scrunchie fabric quietly makes three more things.

Watch for

Leave the 2″ gap. It is not a mistake to tidy up — it is how the elastic goes in and how the tube turns.
A directional print runs AROUND the loop, not up it. If the print has a clear top and bottom, cut along the length instead and accept fewer per yard.
Overlap the elastic ½″ and stitch it hard. A scrunchie only ever fails at that join.

Sewing it ten steps

  1. Cut one strip, 5″ × 26″.
  2. Fold in half widthwise, short ends together, right sides facing. Sew the short ends with a ½″ seam to make a loop. Press the seam open.
  3. Lay the loop flat with the seam centred, right side up.
  4. Fold both long raw edges in to meet at the centre, so the seam is encased. That rolled bundle is the burrito.
  5. Pin the long edge and sew a few inches at a time with a ½″ seam, reaching inside to pull the bunched inner fabric through as you go — this turns the tube from within.
  6. Keep going: sew a bit, pull more out, realign the raw edges, sew again — until about 2″ from where you started.
  7. Backstitch and stop, leaving that 2″ open. Reach through and pull the rest out to turn it fully. Press.
  8. Safety pin on the 10″ elastic and thread it through the gap and all the way round.
  9. Overlap the ends ½″, stitch securely into a loop, and push the join inside the tube.
  10. Tuck the raw edges in and topstitch the gap closed. Scrunch it evenly around the elastic.
