Fabric: ⅞ yd outer · ⅞ yd lining · a fat quarter does the cuff and loop. A fat quarter will NOT do the body — the leg alone is 19″ long once the seam is on.

The shape

Draft it once on paper and keep it. Rule an 8″ × 12″ box for the leg, then draw the foot out from the bottom — 11″ long, about 5″ deep. Round the heel and the toe generously; sharp corners will not turn. Then add ½″ all the way round.

Sewing it about two hours

  1. Loop: fold the strip in half lengthwise, press, fold the raw edges to the middle, press again and topstitch both long sides.
  2. Outers: right sides together, sew all round leaving the top open. Clip the curves at the toe and heel and notch the inner curve at the ankle — skip this and it puckers however well you sewed it. Turn it right side out.
  3. Lining: the same, but leave a 4″ gap in one straight side seam and leave it wrong side out.
  4. Cuff: sew the short ends together into a loop, press the seam open, then fold in half lengthwise wrong sides together so the raw edges meet.
  5. Slide the cuff over the outer, raw edges level with the top, and line its seam up with the back seam of the stocking.
  6. Pin the loop at the back seam, folded in half, raw ends up and sticking out past the top edge.
  7. Drop the whole thing inside the lining, which is still wrong side out, so right sides face. Match the side seams. Pin well — this is four layers and it shifts.
  8. Sew all the way round the top. Go slowly over the loop and the seams.
  9. Turn everything through the gap in the lining, close the gap by hand or edgestitch it, push the lining down inside and fold the cuff over.
  10. Press. A stocking that has been pressed looks bought; one that has not looks homemade.
