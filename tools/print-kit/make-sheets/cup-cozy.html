Cup Cozy

Reusable coffee sleeve · fits 12–20 oz · about 15 minutes
3½″tall
11″top edge
10″bottom edge
¼″seam allowance

Cut list

PieceCutNotes
Outer · coffee print1 × 4″ × 11½″, tapered Take ¼″ off each end of ONE long edge, so it narrows to 10½″. That taper is what hugs the cup.
Lining1, same shape Cut the outer first, then use it as the template.
Fusible fleece or batting1, same shape Fused to the WRONG side of the outer. This is what stops the heat.
Elastic (hair elastic or cord)1 × 3½″ loop Optional. With a button it makes one size fit 12–20 oz.
Button1 · ⅝–¾″ Optional. Or skip both and sew the ends into a fixed ring.

The shape

Sewing it

  1. Fuse the fleece to the wrong side of the outer piece and press.
  2. Pin the elastic (if using) at the centre of one SHORT end, on the right side of the outer, with the loop pointing INWARDS. Baste it down.
  3. Lay the lining on top, right sides together, and sew all the way round with a ¼″ seam, leaving a 2″ gap along one long edge.
  4. Clip the corners, turn it right side out through the gap, and push the corners out with something blunt.
  5. Press well, folding the gap’s raw edges in so they line up with the seam.
  6. Topstitch right round, about ⅛″ from the edge. That closes the gap and gives it a crisp finish.
  7. Sew the button on the other short end, on the outer side, positioned so the elastic pulls the cozy snug on a 16 oz cup. Test it on a real cup before you stitch.
No button version: skip the elastic, and at step 3 leave BOTH short ends open. Turn, press, then overlap the ends by ½″ and stitch a neat rectangle through both. That gives a fixed ring, quicker to make and fine for a standard 16 oz cup, but it will not fit a 20 oz.
