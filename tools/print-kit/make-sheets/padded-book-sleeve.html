Where the numbers come from

Sewing it

  1. Quilt the outers: layer each outer piece over its batting and quilt — straight lines an inch apart, or diagonals. Then trim both down to 7¼″ × 8¾″ so they match the lining.
  2. Test the fit now. Pin the two outers together, slide the device in, and check it goes without forcing. Padding eats more room than you expect, and this is the last moment to change it.
  3. Sew the outer: right sides together, down both sides and across the bottom. Clip the bottom corners.
  4. Sew the lining the same way, but leave a 3″ gap in the bottom seam for turning.
  5. Slide the outer INSIDE the lining, right sides together, match the side seams, and sew right round the top edge.
  6. Pull it all through the gap, close the gap by hand or machine, push the lining down inside, press the top edge and topstitch right round, about ¼″ from the edge.
  7. Press, and check the device one more time before it goes in a bag.
Sizing anything else: inside width = device width + its thickness + ½″ ease. Inside height = device height + half the thickness + ¾″, so it can be pulled out one-handed. Then add ½″ all round for seams, and another ½″ on the outer pieces for quilting take-up. If the finished sleeve is snug, add width, not height. Cassidy’s Kindle measures 6.96 × 5.02 × 0.31 bare, so these numbers suit it IN A CASE — take ¼″ off the width if she says bare.
