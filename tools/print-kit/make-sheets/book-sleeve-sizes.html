The four rules every number below comes from these

W + thicknessinside width
+ ½″ease

THE ONE THAT CATCHES PEOPLE OUT: thickness is part of the WIDTH. A flat sleeve has to wrap around the book, so the inside width is the book’s width plus its spine — not its width. A 2″ romantasy hardcover 6¼″ across needs 8¼″ of inside width, not 6¼″. Get this wrong and it will not go in at all. Height only ever needs ease.

One fat quarter does the outer, one does the lining — for all three sizes. L is tight: lay the two pieces end to end across the 22″ width, not the 18″. Cut the outer and batting BIG and trim after quilting, which is what the extra ½″ is for. Then test the fit before you sew the lining in. Padding eats more room than anyone expects, and that is the last moment it can be changed.

Which size

The Reading Nook Sleeve sits BETWEEN M and L. At 12″ × 8½″ outside it has about 7½″ of inside width — a 6″ hardcover up to roughly 1½″ thick. A 2″ romantasy hardcover will not go in it. Say so when someone asks, or make them an L.
