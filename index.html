Our Story

Every great adventure begins with a single chapter

For me, that chapter started in an unexpected place.

After becoming a mother and navigating postpartum depression, I found myself searching for something that felt like me again. I needed a creative outlet — a place to slow down, work with my hands, and rediscover the joy of making something beautiful. What began as a hobby became a passion. That passion became Dragon Ink and Thread.

Books have always transported me beyond the ordinary — from the moment I first opened Twilight to getting lost in Throne of Glass. They reminded me that even the simplest moments can hold adventure, comfort, and hope. That's the feeling I want to share through everything I make.

Every tote, bow, and scrunchie is handmade in small batches, with thoughtful attention to detail. I never rush the process, because I believe handmade should mean something. When a fabric sells out, its story becomes part of Dragon Ink and Thread's history — making room for the next chapter to begin.

“I want you to feel like this was a missing piece of your wardrobe the whole time.”

You don't have to leave wonder behind just because you've grown up.

Welcome to the Dragon's Nest. There's always room for one more adventurer.

Proof of Work

Find the repair

Linda's family quilt is thirty years old and came to her from her mother. Before it could be passed on again, there was a hole worn clean through where four seams meet near the edge — the cloth gone to lint, the batting coming out.

The mend is in this photograph. Most people can't find it. Matching twenty-year-old cotton for scale, colour and fade is the whole difficulty, and it's the difference between a quilt that gets folded away and one that keeps going.

— Linda M., who can now pass it on to her daughter

Got something worth keeping? Repairs and mending start at $40 — send me a photo and I'll tell you honestly whether it's worth doing.

Frequently asked questions

Are you open? When can I order?

The shop is open now — every ready-made piece is available to order today. Custom orders (personalized, made-just-for-you pieces) open August 17are open too. Join the Nest for 10% off your first purchase — the code runs until August 17.for 15% off your first order over $25.

Is everything really handmade?

Yes — every tote, bow, and scrunchie is handmade in small batches right here in San Antonio, Texas. When a fabric sells out, that print retires and a new one takes its place.

How long until my order ships?

Quickly — everything in the shop is already sewn and ready to ship, so orders usually go out within 1–3 business days. Custom orders are the exception: those are made from scratch for you, and take 10–14 days from the moment we agree on the details, plus shipping — except quilts, which take 8–12 weeks.

How do I care for my pieces?

Totes: spot clean and store flat. Scrunchies: hand wash and lay flat to dry. Bows: spot clean and keep out of direct sun to keep colors true. Full care notes live on each product.

Do you take custom requests?

Yes — custom orders open August 17are open. That page explains what I can make, how pricing works, and has the request form. You can also find me on Instagram @dragonink_and_thread.

Where do you ship, and what does it cost?

Shipping and returns details are on our Shipping & Returns page. Still curious? Just say hello.

Can I pick up locally and skip shipping?

Yes! If you're in the San Antonio area, choose Local pickup at checkout — it drops the shipping fee to $0. We'll email you after your order to arrange a time and place.
