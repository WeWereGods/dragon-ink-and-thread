# Market posts — Sat Sep 26 (evening) · Sat Oct 10 (day) · Fri Oct 30 (night)

**Written 2026-09-13; Sep 26 added 2026-09-14.** Posts to support the three markets in TASKS.md
(🎪 entries) and the printable checklist `marketing/october-market-prep.html`.
⚠️ **Sep 26 is now the FIRST market**, so the October posts no longer say "my first market".

**Why market posts are different from everything else in `single-post-queue.md`:** every post
so far has tried to send people to the website, and none of them sold anything. **A market post
sends people to a table in San Antonio on a date** — local groups allow selling, local people can
actually turn up, and it is the first channel here that ends at a till.

## ⚠️ Before any of these go out

- **Fill every `[BRACKET]`.** Market names and places are not recorded anywhere in this repo
  (deliberately — the repo is public). The times for all three are filled in from the organisers'
  flyers. **Don't post a bracket.**
- **Tag the market and share its own event page.** Most markets repost vendors who tag them, and
  their page has a far bigger local audience than yours. Ask the organiser if they have a vendor
  graphic — it's free reach.
- **Don't invite dogs** unless the market allows them. The posts ask people to *measure* their
  dog instead.
- **Groups:** one post per group per week, **different wording in each**, stagger the days. Same
  text in several groups on one day gets reach suppressed on all of them.
- **Measure it the only way a market can be measured:** ask everyone who buys *"how did you hear
  about me?"* and tally it on the inventory list — **post · group · walked past · friend**.
  Cloudflare can't see a market.
