# Sticker captions — the green-dragon candidate batch

**Written 2026-09-25.** Captions for the 23 designs logged under ⚪ Decisions open in TASKS.md
(a sheet of 21 small ones, *Where the Wild Moss & Little Dragons Grow*, and the Christmas
*Fueled by Cocoa & Dragon Sparks*).

## ⚠️ Before any of these go out

- **NOTHING IS ORDERED YET.** Only **Part A** can be posted now. It asks people which ones to
  print, which is honest about where things stand, and the answers count as demand research for the
  ordering decision in TASKS.md. **Part B says they're on the table, so hold it until a batch has
  actually arrived.**
- **Never say "drawn by me", "hand-drawn" or "my illustrations."** The art reads as AI-generated,
  and a claim like that is the thing that gets called out in a crafting group. The captions below
  talk about *choosing* and *printing* them, which is true.
- **No "shop now" or website link for stickers.** They're not on the website (same rule as the
  current batch). Point people to the table, or to DMs to hold one.
- **Don't name the Pearl or Mercado de Otono** in a sticker post; stickers can't go to either
  without checking first.
- **The dragon is called Willow here**, after the "About me" post of Sep 22. If the sticker dragon
  isn't Willow, swap the name out before posting.
- **Fill every `[BRACKET]`.** Price is `[$3]` because it isn't decided (TASKS.md suggests $3 each,
  maybe more for the two large die-cuts).
