# Single posts — one thing at a time

**Started 2026-08-31**, after the Aug 29 push did not run and the traffic read said why the
big-launch shape was wrong: the email list is 8 people and Pinterest sends nobody, so two of the
push's three legs don't exist. What actually moves this site is **one Facebook post** — an
ordinary one about the free pattern pulled ~20 visits in an afternoon on Aug 25, still the best
day the site has had.

So this file is a queue of standalone posts. **No runbook, no coordination, no clear weekend
required.** One goes out, you watch what happens, the next one goes out.

**How to tell if it worked:** Cloudflare → Referer, and the Path tab. Baseline is ~64–68 visits a
week. See `facebook-groups.md` for the rules that keep you welcome in groups — especially
**never paste the same text into two groups on the same day**, which is why there are three
different versions below rather than one.

### 1b. Quilting / sewing groups — ask, don't tell

*⚠️ **This is the reach lever, and the free pattern is what makes it possible.** Sewing groups
allow process and ban selling — and this post sells nothing at all, so for once there is nothing
to get removed. Same logic as template 3 in `facebook-groups.md`: it asks a real question, and
sewists engage hard with construction questions.*

*Post the photo with it. **Don't put the link in the post** — offer it, and let the group decide.*

> I've been drafting a dog neckerchief and I've written it up to give away, so I've had to
> actually decide things I'd been doing by feel for months.
>
> The one I went back and forth on was the neckband. I've settled on a four-layer folded strip
> with the elastic tucked up inside each end and topstitched through — it presses flat, there's
> no casing to thread, and nothing shifts about in the wash. But plenty of people would sew a
> channel and thread elastic through it instead, and I can see the argument: it's adjustable
> later, and mine isn't.
>
> Which way do you do it, and why? I've a feeling I've talked myself into the harder one.
>
> (It's a free pattern, not a thing I'm selling — happy to drop the link in the comments if
> that's allowed here, or leave it out entirely.)

### Posting notes

- **Not all three on the same day.** Facebook's spam detection keys on repeated content and will
  quietly suppress reach on all of them. Your own page first; a group a day or two later.
- **Reply to every comment quickly.** Early engagement decides how far it travels.
- **Watch the Path tab, not just the total.** The question is whether
  `/pattern-dog-neckerchief.html` gets visits at all, and then whether
  `tools/buttondown-report.js` shows anything under `pattern-neckerchief`.
- ⚠️ **Don't expect shop visits from it.** Measured 2026-08-31: **zero** visits to `shop.html` or
  `custom.html` in a week, from traffic that was almost entirely pattern readers. This post is
  for reach and for the tag experiment. Judging it on sales would be judging it on something a
  free pattern has already been shown not to do.
