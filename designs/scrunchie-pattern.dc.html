Chunky Scrunchie
Beginner Sewing Pattern · Makes 1
Materials
  • Fabric strip, 5in × 26in — quilting cotton is easiest to start with; jersey, satin or velvet once you’re confident
  • Elastic, ⅝in wide, cut to 10in
  • Matching thread
  • Sewing machine (or needle for hand-sewing)
  • Safety pin or bodkin
  • Pins, scissors, iron
¼ yard is plenty. A fat quarter won’t work — it’s only 22in long, and you need 26in.

Sewing Instructions
Burrito method — 10 steps, about 20 minutes.
1
Cut one fabric strip, 5in × 26in.
2
Fold the strip in half widthwise (short ends together), right sides facing. Sew the short ends with a ½in seam to form a loop. Press the seam open.
3
Lay the loop flat with the seam centered, right side facing up.
4
Fold both long raw edges in to meet at the center, encasing the seam inside — this rolled-up bundle is the "burrito."
5
Pin the long edge and sew with a ½in seam a few inches at a time, reaching inside to pull some of the bunched inner fabric through as you go — this turns the tube right side out from within.
6
Keep repeating: sew a bit, pull more of the inner layer out, realign the raw edges, sew again — until you're within about 2in of your starting stitches.
7
Backstitch and stop, leaving that 2in gap open. Reach through it and pull the rest of the fabric out to fully turn the tube right side out. Press.
8
Attach a safety pin to the 10in elastic and thread it through the gap and all the way around the tube.
9
Overlap the elastic ends by ½in and stitch securely to join into a loop, then push the joined elastic inside the tube.
10
Tuck the raw edges of the gap in and topstitch closed. Scrunch the fabric evenly around the elastic. Done!
