## ⚠️ Three decisions baked into the copy

1. **The price is confirmed, not reopened.** $12 each stands. The quote was given, the fabric
   is already bought, and the marginal cost now is thread and an hour. **Re-pricing inside an
   apology email is the worst possible moment to do it** — it reads as "sorry I'm late, and
   it's doubled." The copy states the agreed figure so it can't drift at handover, and goes no
   further. ⛔ **Don't raise it to the $22 floor here.**
2. **It names a date, because "soon" is exactly what cost 28 days.** The copy says
   **Friday 6 November** — after the Oct 30/31 markets, and a day inside the **~Nov 7 sewing
   stop** (the machine is boxed before the **Nov 25** move).
3. ⚠️ **It mentions the relocation in one line.** Not for sympathy — to foreclose
   *"no rush, December is fine."* **December does not exist for this order.** If it slips past
   Nov 25 it stops being a local handover and becomes a shipping problem across state lines.
