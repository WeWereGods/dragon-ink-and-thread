# October organiser — the orphaned $30

A **Facebook/Messenger DM**, not an email. Sent by hand.
**Her name is in the private note, not here** — this repo is public.

## The situation

- **$30 was paid for the Oct 10 booth.** ✅ Paid in full, ahead of time.
- ⛔ **The organiser moved that date**, in her own words: *"not so much cancelled, just had to move
  dates and locations around. The gal in Adkins is not available for October after all."*
- ⛔ **Then she cancelled Oct 31 as well** (2026-10-13): *"I decided to take Halloween off so I can
  take my son out."*
- She offered to **transfer the paid day** to any remaining October, November or December date.
  **Every one of them falls after the Nov 25 move**, so there is nothing to transfer it to.
- ✅ **Oct 30 is separately paid ($30) and still going ahead.** That one is fine.

📌 **The fair outcome is a refund.** The vendor did not withdraw — the organiser changed the dates.

## ⚠️ Why this one needs a different approach

**The refund question has been asked twice and answered neither time.** On both occasions the reply
changed the subject to alternative dates. The last message — *"any update?"* — was **seen on
Wed 2026-10-21 and left unanswered**, six days before this was written.

So the draft below does three things the earlier asks did not:

1. **It asks one binary question** and says a yes or no is fine. A vague ask is easy to leave unread.
2. **It gives the organiser an easy yes that costs her nothing** — see the double-space option below.
3. **It names the deadline** without making it a threat.

⛔ **THE DEADLINE IS REAL: chasing $30 from another state after Nov 25 is the same as writing it off.**

📌 **Keep it warm.** Her register is warm and informal ("Hi mama", "my dear"), **and Oct 30 is still
her market** — the goodwill has to survive Friday. This is not the message to get sharp in.

## 💡 The option worth leading with

**Ask for the $30 to be put toward a DOUBLE SPACE on Oct 30** as the alternative to a refund.

It is the better ask, because:
- **It costs the organiser nothing** — she gives floor space, not cash out of pocket. Far easier to
  say yes to, which is the whole problem here.
- **It converts dead money into frontage on the only Halloween-timed table that still exists.**
  Oct 10 and Oct 31 are both gone; Friday is the lot.
- It keeps the tone collaborative rather than adversarial three days before working together.

> forward to Friday! 🎃

## ⬜ If there's no answer by Thursday 29th

**Ask her in person on Friday**, at check-in, before setting up. Three days of silence on Messenger
is not refusal — it is a busy organiser. **A friendly face-to-face question gets answered when a DM
does not**, and Friday is the last time these two are in the same place.

⚠️ **If the answer is no, take it and let it go.** $30 is not worth souring the last market, and
there is no future relationship to protect once the van leaves — but there is still a Friday night
to trade through.
