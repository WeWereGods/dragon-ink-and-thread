# Booth enquiry — to a market organiser

**Written 2026-09-29.** Reusable. Sent by hand from Gmail, one market at a time.

## Before sending

- **Fill every `[BRACKET]`.** Market names aren't recorded in this repo (it's public).
- **Attach 2–3 photos:** the full table (the evening shot from Sep 26 is the strongest) plus one
  close-up of a bandana or bow. Organisers jury from photos more than from words.
- **Check the dates.** The last San Antonio market is **Nov 14**; the move is **Nov 25**. Don't
  apply for anything after that here.
- **Juried market?** Leave out the stickers. They look AI-generated, and some juried markets ban
  that (see TASKS.md). Everything named in this email is sewn by hand, so it stays true.
- If they use **Marketspread** or their own form, apply there instead. Send this only if they
  ask for an email, or as a short note alongside the form.
